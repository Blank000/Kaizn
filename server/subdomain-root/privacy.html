Zuzu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Zuzu is a habit and milestone tracker. This page explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and how to delete it. In short: your habits, tasks and history stay on your phone unless you back them up to your own Google Drive, and the only things that leave your phone for our server are the AI chat messages you choose to send.

What stays on your phone

Your milestones, tasks, completions, points, streaks, rewards, timer history, Pico chat history and settings are stored in a database on your device. We do not receive or see them.

What leaves your phone, and why

DataSent toWhy
Your Google account name, email and profile picture Google (sign-in) To sign you in. Zuzu shows your name in the app.
A backup copy of your Zuzu data Your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder only Zuzu can read So you can restore your data on a new phone. Only when you are signed in; automatic backups run about once a day.
Messages you send to Pico, the in-app assistant, with a summary of your milestones, tasks and stats, and any photos, files or web-page text you attach Zuzu's AI server, which passes them to OpenAI. If you added your own OpenAI key, they go straight to OpenAI instead. To answer you. Nothing is sent until you press send.
A sign-in token proving which Google account is asking Zuzu's AI server, which checks it with Google So only real Zuzu users can use the free AI allowance, and so it can be counted per person.
A Pico reply you report, and the reason you give Zuzu's AI server So we can review it and improve the assistant.
Calendar events (only if you turn on Google Calendar in Settings) Read from and written to your Google Calendar on your phone To show your calendar beside your tasks.

Web pages you link in Pico are downloaded by your phone, not by our server. Voice input uses your phone's own speech recognition; the audio is not sent to Zuzu or OpenAI.

What Zuzu's AI server keeps

  • Message content: not stored or logged. It is passed to OpenAI and the reply is passed back.
  • Your Google account's ID number and how many messages you sent each day, to enforce the daily limit.
  • A fingerprint (hash) of recent sign-in tokens, so Google is not asked on every message. These expire within about an hour.
  • Replies you report, kept for up to 180 days.

OpenAI processes messages under its API data policy: API data is not used to train its models by default and is kept for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone beyond the services above.
  • No location, contacts or other phone data is collected.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Erase all data. This deletes everything on your phone, your Drive backup, and everything Zuzu's AI server holds about your Google account, then signs you out. It cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app, email us from your Google account's address at CONTACT_EMAIL and we will delete what the server holds within 30 days. You can also remove Zuzu's Drive backup yourself: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Zuzu → Delete hidden app data.

Children

Zuzu is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be mentioned in the app.
