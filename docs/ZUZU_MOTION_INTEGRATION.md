# "A Day With Zuzu" — motion integration spec

*Hand this to an AI along with `docs/AI_CONTEXT.md`. It says what to wire,
exactly where, and the rules that stop celebrations firing when they
shouldn't. Written 2026-09-22.*

---

## 0. SETTLE THIS FIRST — the app currently has two senseis

The storyboard dresses **Zuzu in a sensei's robe, headband and staff**. He
bows to the user and calls them *student*. That is **Master Ren's entire
persona** — Ren is a fox sensei who already owns reflection, rest days,
comebacks, empty states, miss check-ins and the Sunday review.

The written concept keeps Ren for "reflection and setbacks" while making
Zuzu a mentor who bows to you. **Both cannot be the master.** Decide before
wiring moments 1 and 3, because both of those are Ren's surfaces today.

| Option | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Zuzu is the only sensei** *(recommended)* | Ren retires or becomes a background elder. One mentor, who is also the app's name and its icon. This is the Duolingo shape: the brand, the mascot and the voice are one thing. | Ren is wired into ~8 surfaces (`sensei_post.dart`, rest banner, comeback, empty state, miss check-in, review host, stats voice, notification voice, plus a Settings toggle). Real work, but mechanical. |
| **B — Zuzu loses the robe** | Zuzu becomes a companion or fellow student; Ren stays the master. | Contradicts the storyboard that was just drawn and approved. |
| **C — Strict domain split** | Zuzu owns *daily practice* (invitation, first win, day close). Ren owns *weekly reflection and setbacks* only, and becomes rare. | Cheapest, and it is what the written concept describes. Two teachers in one dojo is odd but survivable if Ren is genuinely rare. |

**C is shippable today with no refactor. A is the better brand.** Nothing
below depends on which is chosen except *who appears* in moments 1 and 3.

## 1. What already exists

Three Lottie files are built and in `assets/lottie/`, generated by
`tools/build_zuzu_lottie.py` (edit the generator, never the JSON):

| File | Length | Loop | Moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| `zuzu_invitation.json` | 4.0s, 120f | yes | blink, curious head tilt, wing gesture toward the journal |
| `zuzu_first_win.json` | 1.4s, 42f | no | crouch, leap, two wing flaps, land, settle |
| `zuzu_day_complete.json` | 2.0s, 60f | no | looks down at the list, bows from the feet, rises, waves |

All ~16KB, 512×512, transparent, 30fps, shape layers only. **Frame 0 of each
is Zuzu standing neutral**, which is the reduced-motion requirement: the app
plays Lottie with `animate: !still`, so reduced motion *freezes* rather than
hides.

The drop-in pattern already used by `StreakFlame` is the model to copy —
`Lottie.asset(..., animate: !still, errorBuilder: <drawn fallback>)`. A
missing or unparseable file must never crash a surface.

## 2. Where each one hooks

### Moment 1 — The Invitation
**Trigger:** Home, today has **zero real completions** *and* at least one
scheduled task. Not "no tasks exist" — that is the empty state, a different
thing.

**Place:** `lib/features/home/widgets/sensei_post.dart`, the card Ren
currently fronts. Under option A or C this becomes Zuzu's card for this
state. Keep the existing state-aware copy engine (`RenLines.accountability`)
— it already composes from real data (remaining task names, counts, hour of
day, yesterday's misses). Only the figure and the voice change.

**Loops** quietly. It must not pull focus from the task list.

### Moment 2 — The First Win
**Trigger:** first *real* completion of the day. Skips and honest misses do
not count.

**Place:** `lib/shared/widgets/first_win_ignition.dart` — this already
exists, already fires on exactly this condition, and **already renders a
Lottie**. This is the cheapest of the three: add Zuzu's flight beside the
igniting flame.

The fire keeps the streak; Zuzu supplies the pride. Do not have Zuzu carry
the streak — that is the fire's job.

### Moment 3 — Day Complete
**Trigger:** the last **scheduled** task of the day is completed.

**Place:** `lib/shared/widgets/day_complete_sequence.dart`, appended after
the existing choreography (fire hero, "YATTA!" type, level progress). The
bow is the closing beat, not a replacement for it.

Copy: *"Well done, student. See you tomorrow."* If the day finishes before
the evening band, use *"Today's practice is complete. Enjoy your day."*

## 3. Firing rules — these are the part that gets missed

1. **Play once per day, per moment.** Reopening the app, or undoing and
   re-checking a task, must not replay the flight or the bow. Persist a
   per-day flag in `AppPrefs` keyed by date, in the same style as
   `nmtDismissedDate` and `coachDismissedDate`.
2. **Collapse overlapping wins.** If the first completion is *also* the last
   scheduled task, play **one** compact celebration, not a flight
   immediately followed by a bow.
3. **Anytime tasks are excluded** from "all today's tasks complete". This is
   already true in `home_screen.dart` and must stay true.
4. **A day with no scheduled tasks never triggers victory.** Finishing zero
   of zero is not an achievement.
5. **An honest miss gets warmth, never ignition.** Recording a miss must not
   fire any completion animation. Under option A this is Zuzu with a soft
   nod; under C it stays Ren's miss check-in.
6. **Never block.** No mandatory dismissal, no modal the user must sit
   through. Every one of these must be interruptible by the next tap.
7. **Reduced motion** shows the frozen frame 0 plus the same message. Never
   hide the message because motion is off.

## 4. Build and verify

```
python tools/build_zuzu_lottie.py      # regenerates all three
```

Verify the way the rest of this project does: render real frames and look at
them, at the size they actually ship at. The generator's docstring explains
the layer-parenting technique — two null layers, ROOT at the feet and HEAD
at the neck, with every body part parented to one of them and holding
absolute coordinates. Keyframe transforms on those nulls; never recompute
shape data per frame.

Two bugs already found and fixed, worth knowing because they recur:

- **A scaled layer needs its anchor set.** The eyes blinked by scaling Y,
  but with anchor `[0,0]` they scaled toward the canvas origin and slid off
  the head. Anchor them at the feature's own centre.
- **A rotating limb needs its pivot at the joint.** Wing anchors sit 35px
  above the wing's centre, at the shoulder, or a flap becomes a spin.

## 5. Out of scope for this release

The wider cast mapping in the concept — Ren settling into meditation on
focus start, Pico's thinking lights, Kai's victory pose on milestone
completion, the reward reveal — is deliberately **not** in this release.
Ship the three moments, live with them for a week, then expand. Kai is not
even ported into the app yet.
